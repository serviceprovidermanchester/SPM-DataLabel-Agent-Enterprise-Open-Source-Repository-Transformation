# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

**SPManchester Private Limited** and the maintainers of **DataLabel** are committed to providing an open, welcoming, diverse, and harassment-free environment for all participants, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Expected Standards

We hold all community participants to high professional standards:

- **Respect and Professionalism:** Demonstrating empathy and constructive communication toward community members.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Welcoming diverse viewpoints, feedback, and collaborative technical debates.
- **Accountability:** Accepting responsibility for errors, taking corrective action, and learning from experiences.
- **Focus on the Community:** Prioritizing solutions that benefit users and open-source contributors as a whole.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following actions are strictly prohibited in project spaces and community channels:

- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or disruptive conduct.
- Inappropriate sexual language, imagery, or unwanted advances.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source context.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainers, led by SPManchester Private Limited engineering leadership, are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or offensive.

---

## Reporting & Contact

Incidents of unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the community team at:
- **Email:** [info@spmanchester.com](mailto:info@spmanchester.com)

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
